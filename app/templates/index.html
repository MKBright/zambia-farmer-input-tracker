How the numbers work

  • 1 lima is stored as 0.25 hectares. The page still shows the lima figure you typed.
  • A 50 kg fertilizer bag counts as 50 kilograms. A 10 kg seed bag counts as 10.
  • Yield is harvested kilograms divided by the hectares that already have a harvest.
  • A field with no harvest yet is left out of the yield. A harvest of 0 bags is a failed crop and stays in.
